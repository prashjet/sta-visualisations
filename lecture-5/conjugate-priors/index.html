Conjugate Priors

A conjugate prior gives a posterior in the same distribution family as the prior.

LikelihoodUnknown parameterConjugate priorPosterior
Gaussian (known variance)Location μGaussianGaussian
BinomialSuccess probability pBetaBeta
PoissonRate λGammaGamma
ExponentialRate λGammaGamma
MultinomialCategory probabilitiesDirichletDirichlet

Conjugacy depends on the likelihood and on which parameters are unknown.

Sequential Bayesian updating: the posterior can become the prior for the next observation, remaining in the same distribution family.
