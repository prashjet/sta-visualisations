Likelihood, prior & posterior

The likelihood is a function of μ, not a probability density over μ. Here it is rescaled to unit area for comparison; this preserves its shape and maximum. The model from the lecture

Xᵢ ∼ N(μ, σ²),   i = 1, …, n

P(μ) = N(μ; η, λ²)

L(μ) = P(X | μ) ∝ exp[−n(μ − x̄)² / (2σ²)]

Observations are independent, σ is known, and x̄ = (1/n) ∑ᵢ xᵢ. The displayed likelihood has the shape N(μ; x̄, σ²/n).

P(μ | X) = N(μ; μ̂MAP, V)

V = (n/σ² + 1/λ²)−1

μ̂MAP = (∑ᵢ xᵢ/σ² + η/λ²) / (n/σ² + 1/λ²)
