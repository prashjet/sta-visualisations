Frequentist vs Bayesian Inference

FrequentistBayesian
Unknown parameter θFixed but unknownUncertainty represented by a probability distribution
Observed data xModelled as a realization of random variablesAlso modelled as random; inference conditions on the observed values.
Central mathematical objectSampling distribution of an estimatorPosterior distribution p(θ | x)
Point estimationMaximum likelihood (MLE)Maximum a posteriori (MAP), posterior mean
Interval estimationConfidence intervalCredible interval
Meaning of 95% intervalProcedure covers the true parameter in 95% of repeated experiments95% posterior probability lies within the interval
Prior informationNo probability distribution required for θExplicit prior distribution p(θ) required

Both approaches model data probabilistically and require assumptions. MLE and MAP are examples of point estimators, not exclusive methods.
