What to look for

For independent observations with population mean μ and finite variance σ², the sample mean has mean μ and standard deviation σ/√n. Larger samples reduce its spread. The central limit theorem explains why its distribution approaches a Gaussian as n grows, even for a skewed or bimodal population.

Sample variance uses denominator n − 1, so s and s² require n ≥ 2. For categorical data the statistic is the sample proportion p̂. The normal overlay for x̄ or p̂ uses the theoretical mean and standard error; for s and s² it is fitted to the simulated statistics as a visual comparison.
